A few experiments with this:

1. If I ask a VLM to detect the object triangle (in my head I know what it is, but the llm no) by only saying to the VLM that triangle is present or not. The same way I do that with squares.
    1. One scenario, triangle can be camel, square can be cow.
    2. Another scenario, triangle can be cow, square can be camel.
    3. Another scenario, triangle can be grass, square can be sand.
    4. Another scenario, triangle can be sand, square can be grass.
    
    Also, what is the behavior if I show this to humans and ask the same? The idea here is to remove the prior understanding humans and VLMs have, by guessing what I interpret as triangle or square. I want to argue against IRM or these theories that try to solve this problem without showing to the model training data that follows the test data distribution at all.
